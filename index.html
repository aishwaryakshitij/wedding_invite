A Guide to Our Wedding Day

Monday · 31 August 2026

The day we have been waiting for — here is how it unfolds.

9:30AM

Gowri Prarthana

The bride enters beneath the floral canopy, followed by prayers and traditional blessings for a harmonious married life.

10:00AM

Baraat

The groom arrives with the Baraat and is ceremonially welcomed by the bride’s family before entering the mandap.

10:15AM

Ganapati Pooja

Both families begin the wedding rituals by seeking Lord Ganesha’s blessings for an auspicious start.

10:30AM

Maharashtrian Wedding Ceremony

Antarpat, where the couple is separated by a curtain until the auspicious moment, followed by their first exchange of garlands.

12:00PM

Tamil Wedding Ceremony

The tying of the Thali, sacred rounds, Saptapadi, and family blessings.

12:45PM

Wedding Lunch

Guests gather for lunch as the morning wedding ceremonies conclude.

5:00PM

Reception

An evening celebration with family and friends to celebrate the newlyweds.
